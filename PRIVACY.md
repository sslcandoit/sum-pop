# Privacy Policy — Sum Pop (凑凑数)

*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

Sum Pop does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up or login.
- **Only one kind of network request: checking for game updates.** When the app opens, it downloads a small file from GitHub (github.com and its download servers) to see whether new levels or fixes are available, and if so downloads them. These requests send nothing about you or your progress. Like any website, GitHub can see the IP address the request comes from; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). If the device is offline, the game works normally without updates.
- **No ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting,** and no third-party services or SDKs that collect data.
- **Data stays on your device.** Your progress (stars and best scores per stage) and your settings (language and sound) are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with **Reset progress** on the home screen, or by deleting the app.
- **Children.** The game is suitable for children. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is collected either.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sslcandoit/sum-pop/issues).

# 隐私政策 — 凑凑数（Sum Pop）

*最后更新：2026 年 11 月 11 日*

凑凑数不收集任何个人信息，不把任何信息存到服务器上，也不与任何人分享。

- **不需要账号。** 没有注册，也没有登录。
- **只有一种联网：检查游戏更新。** 打开 App 时，会从 GitHub（github.com 及其下载服务器）下载一个很小的文件，看看有没有新的关卡或修正；有的话就下载下来。这些请求不包含任何关于你或你的游戏进度的信息。和访问任何网站一样，GitHub 能看到请求来自哪个 IP 地址，详见 [GitHub 隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。没有网络时，游戏照常可以玩，只是不会更新。
- **没有广告、统计分析、追踪或崩溃上报，**也没有包含任何会收集数据的第三方服务或 SDK。
- **数据只保存在你的设备上。** 游戏进度（每关的星星和最高纪录）和设置（语言、音效）只保存在本机 App 的本地存储里，不会离开你的设备。你可以随时在首页点"清除进度"删除这些数据，删除 App 也会一并删除。
- **儿童。** 本游戏适合儿童。因为不收集任何数据，所以也不会收集儿童的个人信息。

如果本政策有变化，更新后的版本会发布在本页面，并标注新的日期。

**联系方式：** 对本政策有疑问，可以通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sslcandoit/sum-pop/issues) 联系。
